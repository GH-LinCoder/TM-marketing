The first step in creating your organization is to apply for your own independent system at http://myorg.netlify.app During the current test period you need approval to obtain your system. This is free and does not need a credit card. You will then receive notification that you have your own database and hosting from suppliers. The accounts there will be in your name. (See pricing for details) You can supply a logo or use the default logo supplied to you. You need to define the name of the organisation. This should be short. Initially as an extra service during the test period the developer of the software will act as an admin on your system. Your initial steps are to open the 'Founders Task' inside your new system dashboard and follow the steps to build the foundations of your organization. These steps are To open and edit the template data of aims plans policies and then open and edit the template Welcome Task which is what all your new recruits will see first. This only needs light editing to explain your organization. Then think about what you want to ask your new recruits and how you want to classify them or what groups you want to assign them to. Then open the template of the 'Initial Survey' and adjust this to fit your needs. This survey is where your new recruits will answer questions and choose what they want to do. The survey will automatically put them into whichever groups you have created and which your recruit chooses to join. The survey can also assign the new person to a task or survey. Using such surveys and tasks means you can automate much of the initial activity of your growing organization.

Each customer's database is independent. The customer has their own Supabase control panel and control over their database. The HQ instance is running the same software as customer instances. HQ cannot see, manipulate or silently intervene in a customer's database. HQ cannot shadow-ban a customer through control of their organisation's data. The customer can voluntarily give HQ administrative access if they choose. Customers have dependencies, but those dependencies are not equivalent to HQ having control over their organisation.

After applying for your independent system you will have gone through the founder's task to put in place the essential foundations of your organization. Then you will have put effort into recruiting. This could be by inviting your existing contacts or audience, or by posting invitattions using a generated referral link. Building from there depends on your choices of what you want to build. Do you need branches? Do you want departments? Do you want interest groups? Do you want to create training courses? What feedback do you want? What choices do you want to offer your participants? What fees or payments, if any, do you want to collect? Who do you want to have permission to do what? Do you want to add bulk email (needs a domain name and it si best to use an integrated supplier) You build structure with tasks & surveys that are automated into funnels and workflows plus by relating participants and tasks and surveys and groups and lots of other things. Anything inside or outside the organization can be represented by a row in a database. We call these 'appros' and they can be use to represent anything. Every person has an appro representing that person, so does every task and every survey. They are all represented by appros, but so are branches or groups or even ideas. Any appro can be related to any other appro to represent the structure of the organization.

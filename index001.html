Core Tools Included in the System

Every independent system comes equipped with a unified set of foundational tools designed to handle the core operational needs of any group:

Membership Stored in a Database

Keep all member records, roles, and statuses in a structured, searchable database instead of scattered files.

Assign and Track Tasks

Delegate concrete actions to members or groups and track progress through to completion.

Get Feedback from Surveys

Run surveys to gather member input, measure consensus, and connect responses into automated workflows.

Build and View Organization Structure

Map out committees, working groups, and reporting relationships so everyone sees how the organization fits together.

Create Referral Links for Recruitment

Equip members with trackable referral links to recruit new participants and measure outreach.

Automated Onboarding Funnels

Guide new visitors automatically based on their intent—whether joining as a member, organizer, developer, or tester.

Internal Messaging

Communicate directly within your organization's platform without relying on disconnected third-party chat tools.

Publish Aims, Plans, Decisions & Policies

Maintain an authoritative record of your mission, strategic plans, governance decisions, and rules.

Accept Payments via a Merchant of Account

Collect membership dues, fees, or contributions through an integrated Merchant of Account setup.

Activity-Based Permission System

Control every tool, record, and action through a granular permission system tailored to each member's role.

Context-Based Help

Integrated, context-sensitive help explains each feature right where it is used so new members need minimal training.

Independent System Websites

Run your organization on its own dedicated, white-label system free from platform marketing clutter.

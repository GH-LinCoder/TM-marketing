Activist Cadre & Skunk Works

Big movements and organisations rarely move fast through committee meetings. Small, high-trust units—activist cadres that execute on the ground and skunk works teams that test new ideas fast—do the heavy lifting when given clear structure and scoped permissions.

Focused Units With Real Autonomy

Give small, committed teams clear objectives and bounded authority without slowing everyone else down.

How Skunk Works and Activist Cadres Work Together

An activist cadre is a compact group of reliable, active members who take on consistent responsibilities—such as local outreach, member onboarding, event logistics, or campaign follow-through—rather than waiting to be asked once in a while.

A skunk works unit is a small, fast-moving group tasked with solving a specific bottleneck, testing a new approach, or building an early pilot with minimal red tape before rolling lessons out more widely.

  • Scoped permissions rather than all-or-nothing trust: Give cadre or skunk works members access to the tasks, records and workflows they need without exposing sensitive data or risking accidental damage across the whole system.
  • Repeatable onboarding and training: Move new recruits from initial interest to active contribution using structured multi-step tasks and clear expectations.
  • Visible progress without micromanagement: Track active tasks, handoffs and milestones in one shared place so leads stay informed while small teams keep moving.

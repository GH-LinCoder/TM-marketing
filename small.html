The power of small

Now small can be powerful

How even the baby now has access to powerful tools

Now even a new very small organisation can have powerful tools that are very much better than using a spreadsheet. Probably the most common tools used by a new or very small organization are a spreadsheet and a social media chat group. Now even the smallest of organizations can make use of powerful tools to start the organization.

These tools include a database of members, recruitment referral links to be posted on social media or in emails that tracks who recruited the new person, Automated onboarding Automated choices that the new person can make over what to do or what group to join Training courses Todo lists Tasks that are to be assigned or repeated Surveys for feedback and member choices Creation of groups or branches Controlling who has permission to do things A management system designed to cope with part-time volunteers Visible maps of the organization, who belongs where and what they are working on Payment processing

  • Automated onboarding: Let the new recruits be funnelled by your system.
  • Recruitment tracking: Know who and what brings in new recruits
  • Automate feedback: Create surveys to know what your members want & let them make choices.

Put It Into Practice

If you have decided that you would like to have your own independent organization system you can click the button below. That will take you to system HQ. It is the same system that you will have, and it is the system that runs the HQ. You just need an email address. You will be asked to pick a user name and a password. If your organization has a name it helps to put that in your user name. The system will then do the auto onboarding and it will let you identify yourself as a potential customer. In the current testing period there will be a manual (human) process to decide whether to grant you a free system of your own.

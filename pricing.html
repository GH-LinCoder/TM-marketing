Test Tier - free The system is under test. You can apply for a free system. If granted you will not be charged anything by the system. This is in exchange for taking part in the testing. You will have use of the software without charge. You will have an independent database and webhosting in your own name. The database is hosted by Supabase. The web hosting is by Netlify. Supabase and Netlfiy have their own terms and conditions and currently offer a free tier which is what you would use. If your organization becomes very busy you may find that either of those two suppliers will limit your access or expect you to upgrade to a paid tier. The current test tier has no charge for the use of the software. Domain Tier - free If you want to send bulk emails to your participants you will need to have a registered domain. This is a requirement of the email system. You can then have an outside supplier of bulk email services connected to the system. A domain can be attached to the website via the Netlify control panel. Our system does not need to be altered to handle the domain. Our system does need to be altered to integrate the choice of bulk email provider. Bespoke Tier - cost to be agreed If you want the system dashboards to be changed such as styles, colors or wording, this would require coding and would be subject to agreement. If you want to integrate a different payment processor or to include any upgrades or addons, these would be subject to agreement

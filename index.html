Create your organization

Whether you already have a following that you want to organise, or you are starting a new club, community or movement, this system provides the tools to build and manage an organisation that can get things done.

As groups grow, informal arrangements become increasingly difficult to manage. Membership lists, spreadsheets, social media and messaging apps can help initially, but they don't provide an integrated way to organise people, responsibilities, activities and feedback.

This is a database-driven system designed to bring those activities together, giving your organisation the structure and tools it needs to develop.

Everything Your Organisation Needs to Function

The system brings together the main activities involved in building and running an organisation.

  • Membership: Store and manage member information in a database.
  • Organisational structure: Define relationships, teams, roles and responsibilities, and display the organisation's structure.
  • Tasks: Assign activities and responsibilities, and track progress.
  • Surveys and feedback: Ask members for information, opinions and feedback.
  • Automated workflows: Connect tasks, surveys and other activities into organised processes.
  • Recruitment: Create referral links and track recruitment activity.
  • Automated onboarding: Guide new participants through appropriate joining and introductory processes.
  • Internal messaging: Communicate with other members within the system.
  • Aims, plans and policies: Publish organisational aims, plans, decisions and policies for members.
  • Payments: Accept fees and other payments through your organisation's payment arrangements.
  • Permissions: Control access to individual activities and functions according to assigned permissions.
  • Context-sensitive help: Get help explaining the features where you are using them.

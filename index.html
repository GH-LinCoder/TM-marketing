What Your Organisation Needs

Every organization is different, but every organization shares foundational needs. The system aims to provide the foundational needs of all organizations.

  • Aims, plans and policies:

    The first steps in creating your new organization are to publish your organisational aims, plans, and policies. Everyone has direct access to these when using the system.
  • Membership:

    Store and manage member information securely in a dedicated database.
  • Automated onboarding:

    New recruits see a 'welcome' task explaining the system, followed by an initial survey that automatically adjusts their experience based on their choices.
  • Surveys and feedback:

    Produce custom surveys to gather member input. Every answer can trigger automated reactions, handling routine clerical actions without your attention.
  • Tasks:

    Create and assign multi-step tasks. Use them as to-do lists, training courses with embedded video, or soft production lines with automated step triggers.
  • Automated workflows:

    Connect tasks, surveys, and structural changes into organized, automated processes that run in the background.
  • Organisational structure:

    Define relationships, teams, roles, and permissions using 'appro' name-tags, and visually display your organization's structure as it grows.
  • Recruitment tracking:

    Generate referral links attached to specific 'appro' tags to track exactly who recruited whom, or which social media channel drove a new member.
  • Internal messaging:

    Communicate within the system, keep personal notes, or send messages directly to a specific task, survey, or group.
  • Payments:

    Accept fees and memberships through integrated payment processors, with funds routing directly to your organization's bank account.
  • Strict Permissions:

    Dual-layer permission systems protect private data and prevent unauthorized access to the database, designed specifically for volunteer administrators.
  • Context-aware help:

    A single 'How?' button opens an explanation of whatever module you currently have open, updating dynamically as you navigate.

The system is categorized as an organisation operating system. This is a new category of software. It provides the foundational needs of almost every kind of organization. What it leaves the existing software companies to is adding apps to handle the differences of organizations. The concept is that every organization would have an organization operating system and on top of that would add apps for their specific needs such as an app to handle sports or an app for first-aid groups.

How Do You Get Started?

Whether you are starting with an idea, bringing people together around a common cause, or turning an existing audience into an active organisation, you can apply for your own independent system.

  1. Apply for your own independent system website. This is done on the HQ website.
  2. Establish your initial organisational structure and permissions.
  3. Publish your aims, plans and policies.
  4. Set up membership, recruitment and onboarding.
  5. Generate referral links & begin recruiting.
  6. Monitor the automated onboarding.

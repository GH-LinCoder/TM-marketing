A system to create, build and run any kind of Organization

This web based system allows anyone to build and manage an organization of any kind. It provides membership management, organizational structures, task management, surveys, automated onboarding, recruitment tools, display of organizational information, internal messaging, automated workflows, payment processing and activity-based access permissions. It has integrated, context-sensitive help.

Two Ways to Use the Platform

1. Build From Scratch

Organize a new club, community, or pressure group. Establish a clear membership database, assign initial tasks, publish your aims, and set up your group structure from day one.

Read about creating an organization →

2. Convert an Audience to Activists

If you already have a following, use the platform to move passive supporters off social feeds and into an active, structured community using tasks, surveys, and trackable recruitment links.

System Structure

  • Create: How you get your own independent system instance and what accounts it depends on. (Create, Who It's For, About)
  • Build: Using tasks, surveys, organizational relationships, referral recruitment, and payment processing. (Build, Turn Audience to Activists, Recruitment, Income & Payments)
  • Run: Day-to-day governance, activity-based permissions, internal messaging, and publishing aims, plans, decisions, and policies. (Run, Operating System, Spreadsheets, Pricing)

Core System Capabilities

  • Membership stored in a database: Structured member records and roles.
  • Assign and track tasks: Task delegation and progress tracking across the group.
  • Get feedback from surveys: Surveys and automated funnels connecting tasks and surveys.
  • Build and view organisation structure: Define and display committees, teams, and relationships.
  • Create referral links for recruitment: Trackable referral links for recruitment and marketing.
  • Automated onboarding: Segments new visitors based on whether they are joining as members, organizers, developers, or testers.
  • Internal messaging: Built-in communication between members.
  • Publish aims, plans, decisions & policies: Display official organizational information in one place.
  • Accept payments through a Merchant of Account: Integrated fee and payment processing.
  • Activity-based permission system: Access permissions controlling every tool and action.
  • Context-based help: Integrated help explaining features where they are used.

Structure Without Bureaucracy

Give your members clear roles, guided onboarding and coordinated tasks on your own independent system.

How the System Helps

Most groups start with enthusiasm and quickly bog down in manual coordination. One person ends up holding all the contacts, chasing volunteers by email and tracking progress in spreadsheets that nobody else can safely update.

An Organisation Operating System gives your group a single, structured home where responsibility can be shared safely as you grow:

  • Clear permissions: Delegate tasks and administrative roles without giving everyone unrestricted access to your whole database.
  • Guided onboarding: Bring new people in through referral links and automated steps that match them to the right role.
  • Coordinated action: Break complex work into multi-step tasks, track progress and keep records in one place.

Put It Into Practice

If you have decided that you would like to have your own independent organization system you can click the button below. That will take you to system HQ. It is the same system that you will have, and it is the system that runs the HQ. You just need an email address. You will be asked to pick a user name and a password. If your organization has a name it helps to put that in your user name. The system will then do the auto onboarding and it will let you identify yourself as a potential customer. In the current testing period there will be a manual (human) process to decide whether to grant you a free system of your own.

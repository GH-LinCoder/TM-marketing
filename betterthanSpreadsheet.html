How even a new very small organisation can have powerful tools that are very much better than using a spreadsheet. Probably the most common tools used by a new or very small organization are a spreadsheet and a social media chat group. Now even the smallest of organizartions can make use of powerful tools to start the organization. These tools include a database of members, recruitment referral links to be posted on social media or in emails that tracks who recruited the new person, Automated onboarding Automated choices that the new person can make over what to do or what group to join Training courses Todo lists Tasks that are to be assigned or repeated Surveys for feedback and member choices Creation of groups or branches Controlling who has permission to do things A management system designed to cope with part-time volunteers Visible maps of the organization, who belongs where and what they are working on Payment processing
